# Dynamics Formulas

## Newton's Laws
- 1st Law: ΣF = 0 → constant velocity
- 2nd Law: F = m · a
- 3rd Law: F_AB = -F_BA

## Work & Energy
- W = F · d · cos(θ)
- Ek = ½ · m · v²
- Ep = m · g · h
- Conservation: Ek1 + Ep1 = Ek2 + Ep2

## Power
- P = W / t
- P = F · v

## Friction
- f = μ · N
- μs > μk (static > kinetic)

## Key Questions to Ask
- What forces act on the object?
- Is the system in equilibrium (ΣF = 0)?
- Is energy conserved?
