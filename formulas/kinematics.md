# Kinematics Formulas

## Uniform Motion (MRU)
- v = s / t
- s = v · t

## Uniformly Accelerated Motion (MRUA)
- v = v₀ + a · t
- s = v₀·t + ½·a·t²
- v² = v₀² + 2·a·s

## Free Fall
- h = ½ · g · t²   (g = 9.81 m/s²)
- v = g · t

## Projectile Motion
- x = v₀·cos(θ)·t
- y = v₀·sin(θ)·t - ½·g·t²

## Key Questions to Ask
- What is known? (v₀, a, t, s)
- What is unknown?
- Which formula connects knowns to unknown?
