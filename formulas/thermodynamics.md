# Thermodynamics Formulas

## Temperature
- T(K) = T(°C) + 273.15

## Heat Transfer
- Q = m · c · ΔT
- c_water = 4186 J/(kg·K)

## Ideal Gas Law
- p · V = n · R · T
- R = 8.314 J/(mol·K)

## Thermal Expansion
- ΔL = α · L₀ · ΔT (linear)
- ΔV = β · V₀ · ΔT (volumetric)

## Key Questions to Ask
- What type of process? (isothermal, isobaric, isochoric)
- What is the system boundary?
- Is heat added or removed?
