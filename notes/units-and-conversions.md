# SI Units & Conversions

## Base SI Units
| Quantity     | Unit     | Symbol |
|-------------|----------|--------|
| Length      | metre    | m      |
| Mass        | kilogram | kg     |
| Time        | second   | s      |
| Temperature | kelvin   | K      |
| Force       | newton   | N      |
| Energy      | joule    | J      |
| Power       | watt     | W      |
| Pressure    | pascal   | Pa     |

## Common Conversions
- 1 km = 1000 m
- 1 h = 3600 s
- 1 kJ = 1000 J
- 1 bar = 100,000 Pa
- 1 N = 1 kg·m/s²
